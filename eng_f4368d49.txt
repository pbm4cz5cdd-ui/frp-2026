## Undergraduate English Requirement

[Full-time Academic English Requirement for Graduate Students](https://www.wichita.edu/admissions/graduate/english-proficiency.php)

[Half-time Academic/Intensive English Study for Graduate Students](https://www.wichita.edu/admissions/international/halftime_grad.php)

*   Conditional Admission is available for students who meet our academic requirements for admission. If you receive conditional admission the major listed on your I-20 will be Intensive English. We will note that you also have admission to an academic program on your I-20.
*   You do not need to submit a TOEFL or IELTS score when you apply.
*   You may send your official score later or we will test your English after you arrive.
*   Students who do not meet our English requirement or do not submit an English score will be required to enroll in the Intensive English Language Center.

### Send Official Results

*   Tests must have been taken within the last two years.
*   When [applying for admission](https://www.wichita.edu/admissions/international/toefl_ielts_req.php), students should upload their test scores with their transcripts and other application materials. If a copy is uploaded with application materials, the scores will be verified with ETS or IELTS.
*   TOEFL scores can be sent from the [Educational Testing Service (ETS)](https://www.ets.org/toefl). Official copies are not required as long as the student copy can be verified.
*   IELTS Academic scores can be sent from an [IELTS Testing Center](https://www.ielts.org/).

### English Proficiency Examination (EPE)

*   Required for students who have not met our English requirement by the time they come to Wichita
*   Administered by Wichita State University
*   Cost is US$75.

### [](https://www.wichita.edu/admissions/international/toefl_ielts_req.php)Intensive English Language Center (IELC)

*   Enrollment in Intensive English is required for students who have not met our English requirement.
*   Students with undergraduate admission or graduate admission may enroll in both Intensive English and academic classes if:
a) Student places in one of the two highest levels of the Intensive English Langauge Program.  
**OR**  
b) Student has an eligible half-time academic score:

    *   TOEFL (iBT) score of 61 to 71
    *   IELTS overall band score of 5.5
    *   PTE Academic score of 40 to 48
    *   Duolingo English Test score of 95 to 104
    *   WSU English Proficiency Examination (EPE) result of 75 to 79
